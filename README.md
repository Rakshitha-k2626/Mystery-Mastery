# 🔍 Mystery Mastery

**Run the city. Solve mysteries. Crack the vault code.**

Mystery Mastery is a neon-themed puzzle runner that combines fast-paced gameplay, mystery questions, programming challenges, and code-breaking puzzles. Choose your detective, race through the city, answer questions, collect rewards, and unlock mystery vaults!

## 🎮 About the Game

Pick your detective and dash through a neon city filled with obstacles, mystery gates, and hidden rewards. Switch lanes, jump over barriers, and slide under bars as you race toward your next challenge.

Choose the lane containing the correct answer to win a mystery box. Then put your problem-solving skills to the test and crack the vault to discover your prize.

## ✨ Key Features

- **🏃 Endless 3-Lane Runner:** Switch lanes, jump, and slide to avoid obstacles.
- **🧩 300 Mystery Questions:** Explore a collection of 300 questions that do not repeat until you've seen them all.
- **🕵️ Three Playable Characters:** Choose your detective, each with unique perks.
- **🔐 Three Vault-Solving Modes:** Solve puzzles your way.
- **🎁 Mystery Rewards:** Discover prizes ranging from Common to Legendary.
- **⭐ Progression System:** Earn coins, advance through levels, and collect star ratings.
- **📴 Offline Gameplay:** Play without an internet connection.
- **🚫 No Ads or Accounts:** Enjoy the game without advertisements or account registration.

## 🔐 Crack the Vault

Mystery Mastery offers three ways to solve the vault puzzle.

### 1. Manual Mode 🧠

Use the available clues and colour-coded feedback to deduce the secret code. Apply logic, test possibilities, and unlock the vault.

### 2. Coding Mode 💻

Read a short Python or C program, determine its output, and use the correct answer to unlock the mystery box. This mode combines programming knowledge with puzzle-solving.

### 3. Ask AI 🤖

Interact with the Vault AI by entering a prompt and receiving a clue to help you solve the mystery.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Select your favourite detective.
2. Start running through the neon city.
3. Switch lanes, jump over barriers, and slide under obstacles.
4. Choose the lane containing the correct answer at a mystery gate.
5. Win a mystery box and enter the vault.
6. Select Manual, Coding, or Ask AI mode.
7. Solve the challenge to unlock your reward.
8. Earn coins, collect prizes, and progress through the game.
